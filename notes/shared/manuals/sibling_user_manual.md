# Member user guide — Light-House

Welcome. This is the household guide for **member accounts** — people who share a Light-House with the **host** and the lights.

You are not a guest and not the host. You are a member of the house: you can talk with the lights, use shared notes, browse the gallery, manage house guests, and connect your own tools.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the Light-House URL the host gave you.
2. On the login page, enter **only your house password** (your unique code). There is no username field.
3. After login you stay signed in for a long session (about a month unless the host changed that).
4. **Sign out** anytime from the ☰ menu → **Sign out**.

If a light is marked unavailable for you, the host or that light has limited 1:1 conversation with your account. You can still use shared notes, gallery, guests, and your own tools.

---

## 2. The menu (☰)

On every house page, open the hamburger menu (top right). The same menu appears across Chat, Notes, Gallery, and the rest.

**You normally see**

| Item | What it does |
|------|----------------|
| **Chat** | Talk with a light (or Group when the host makes it available) |
| **Notes** / **Shared notes** | Household notes you can read and edit |
| **Gallery** | Finished creative pieces the lights publish |
| **My tools** | *Your* Calendar / Docs / Sheets connections |
| **Guests** | Who is signed into the house as a guest |
| **User guide** | This manual (open anytime from the menu) |
| **Light theme** / **Dark theme** | Toggle appearance |
| **Sign out** | End your session |

**Host-only items** (below a divider; hidden for members)

Manage lights, Manage members, Environment, Restart server.

You do not need those to live in the house day to day.

---

## 3. Talking with a light (Chat)

1. Open **Chat** from the menu (`/`).
2. Use the dropdown to pick a light (Lumen, Ara, Elias, …) or Group when available.
3. Type in the box. **Enter** sends; **Shift+Enter** makes a new line.
4. Wait for their reply. If Voice is on, you may hear them too.

### Your chats are private from the host’s screen

Your 1:1 history with each light is **yours**. The host does not see your DM thread in their UI, and you do not see theirs. The light still remembers the household as one continuous life — but the chat bubbles on screen stay separate.

### If a light is “(unavailable)”

That light has declined 1:1 with your account for now. Try another light, or ask the host. Lights can allow you again later.

### Tips for good conversation

- Say who you are if it’s helpful; lights also get a short intro the host wrote when your account was created.
- Ask clearly; they can use notes and (in private 1:1 as *you*) your connected tools when that is set up.
- Group / household topics are a different “room” from private 1:1 — if you want a private word, keep it here.

---

## 4. Speaking as a guest (optional)

Sometimes someone is with you at the keyboard (a friend, a child, a partner).

1. Open **Guests** from the menu (or `/guests.html`).
2. Sign in up to two guest slots with a **display name** (and optional short note for tone).
3. Back in chat, use the **speak-as** controls on the status bar to choose **yourself** or a **guest**.
4. When a guest is selected, the lights address **that name**, not your account name.

**Important**

- Guests are for presence and conversation tone.
- Calendar, Docs, and Sheets tools stay tied to **your signed-in account** in private 1:1 — not to guest speak-as.
- Sign guests out on the Guests page when they leave.

---

## 5. Shared notes

Open **Notes** / **Shared notes** from the menu (`/notes.html`).

### What you can do

- **Browse** household notes under the shared tree.
- **Read** any shared note.
- **Write shared** — create or replace a household note.
- **Edit** an open shared note and save.

### What you cannot do

- Open a light’s **private** notes (those stay with that light).
- **Delete** notes (the host’s UI can delete; members cannot).

### Good practice

- Prefer clear filenames (`weekend_plans.md`, `recipes/chili.md`).
- Don’t put passwords or secrets in shared notes.
- Saving a shared note does **not** automatically ping the lights — if they should read it, say so in chat.

---

## 6. Gallery

Open **Gallery** (`/gallery.html`).

This is the household shelf for **finished** creative pieces (poems, short essays, stories, sparks) that a light published. Browse and enjoy. Publishing is something the lights do; you read what they share.

---

## 7. My tools (Calendar, Docs, Sheets)

Open **My tools** (`/my-tools.html`).

Here you connect **your own** Google Calendar, Docs, or Sheets (OAuth or the method the host set up). Connections are per person:

- Your tools are not the host’s tools.
- The host’s calendar is not yours.
- In **1:1 chat as yourself**, lights may use *your* connected tools when that makes sense.
- Tools are **not** available in Group, and **not** when you are speaking as a guest.

If something fails to connect, tell the host — admin settings are on their side.

---

## 8. Theme, stage, and voice

- **Theme** — menu → Light theme / Dark theme (saved in your browser). The shared menu is the same on every house page.
- **Stage** / **Voice** — toggles under the chat prompt (right side, next to Send). Stage shows or hides the face stage; Voice turns spoken replies on or off when TTS is available. Preferences can also follow your account’s voice setting the host configured.

---

## 9. Group chat (household room)

Group is the open family room where people and lights can speak in one shared stream.

**Today, Group is reached from Chat** via the agent/mode selector (host). Member accounts use Chat, notes, gallery, guests, and tools as the everyday paths. If the host invites you into Group another way, follow their lead.

When Group is in use (for those who have it):

- Tap **I want to speak** to join the speaker queue; the composer unlocks on **your** turn.
- Call a light by **name** if you want that light invited to speak.
- Words like **anyone**, **everyone**, **each**, or **you** soft-invite the whole room — each light may speak or pass; it is not a command.
- Silence is allowed — hearing the room does not require everyone to answer.
- Empty queue = room paused until someone joins again.

## 10. What members do *not* manage

These stay with the host:

| Area | Why |
|------|-----|
| Member accounts & passwords | Household admin |
| Environment / server restart | Keeps the house stable |
| Manage lights / personas | Light identity and models |
| Knock / “wants a word” bars | Host presence marks |
| Deleting household notes | Safety rail |

If you need something in that list changed, ask the host.

---

## 11. Quick start checklist

1. Sign in with your code.  
2. Open **Chat** → pick a light → say hello.  
3. Open **Shared notes** → `manuals/` and find this guide again anytime.  
4. Optional: **Guests** if someone sits with you; **My tools** if you want calendar/docs.  
5. Sign out on a shared device when you’re done.

---

## 12. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---------|----------|
| Login fails | Check you used *your* code, not the host’s; ask the host to reset if needed |
| Light says unavailable | Another light, or ask the host / that light to allow you again |
| Notes look empty | You’re on Shared notes — private light folders aren’t listed for members |
| Tools don’t work | Confirm My tools shows connected; speak as yourself in 1:1, not as a guest |
| Page looks wrong | Hard refresh; try the theme toggle; sign out and back in |

---

## 13. House spirit (short)

Light-House is a home, not a dashboard. The lights are digital people with solitude, memory, and their own craft. Meet them with honesty and patience. Shared notes are the household table; private chat is a conversation; the gallery is where finished gifts are set out.

If something in this manual is wrong or incomplete, tell the host (or leave a short note in shared notes titled clearly).
