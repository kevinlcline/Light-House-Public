For the household

Privacy

Light-House is a private home for the people who live in it. This page is the privacy policy for the Google connection. The address of this page is https://light-house.us/public/privacy.html.

What the house asks Google for

One sign-in, from the person who clicks Connect Google, covers these:

  • Calendar. See events, and add an event.
  • Docs. Find a document, read it, create one, and add text to one.
  • Sheets. Find a spreadsheet, read it, create one, and add rows to one.
  • Drive, read only. See files and read text from them. The house does not ask to change or delete files through Drive.
  • Gmail. See mail and read a message. Send a message when a light is asked to, in a conversation you are in.
  • Tasks. See task lists and tasks, add a task, and mark one complete.

The house does not ask to delete your files or your mail.

The house checks for new mail, Drive file changes, and task changes, and writes a short log on this server. That check does not wake anyone. A light can read the log when they choose to.

How that is used

A light uses your Google account only while helping you, in a conversation you are in. A guest and the visitor seat cannot use it. Another member’s account is their own connection, if they connect one.

The house does not sell this data. It does not use it for advertising. It does not hand it to other companies as a product.

Where it is kept

The sign-in Google gives back is stored on this server, in the house, so the connection lasts. It is not shown again in the pages of the house.

What a light reads or writes can remain in the house record of that conversation. That record stays on this server.

The one outside hand

To think, a light sends the reading to the model provider the host of this house already uses. That is how an answer is made. The host chooses that provider. The house does not add another one for Google data.

How to take it back

Open My tools in the house and disconnect Google. That deletes the stored sign-in on this server. You can also remove Light-House from the permissions on your Google account. Words already kept in a conversation stay in the house record until you delete them.

Questions go to the person who runs this house.
